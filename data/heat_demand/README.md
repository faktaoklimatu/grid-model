The heat demand data come from ERÚ annual reports. The `monthly_demand_TJ` column
approximates monthly production of heat from CHP plants. It was originally
calculated as 64% of net heat production in each month. 64% is the average share
of CHP on heat production throughout the year. It has now been rescaled (by
another approx. 66% down) so as to roughly match expected demand in 2030 and
2035 per NECP.
